# 🗺️ AI Lead Generation Automation

An AI-powered lead generation automation built with **n8n** that finds relevant business leads from Google Maps based on a target location and business type, then organizes the collected data into a structured lead list.

## 🚀 Overview

Finding potential business leads manually from Google Maps can be repetitive and time-consuming.

This automation turns the process into a structured workflow:

**Business Type + Location → Google Maps → Lead Extraction → Data Processing → Structured Lead List**

The goal is to reduce manual prospecting work and give sales teams a ready-to-use list of potential leads.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔎 Search businesses by location and business type
- 📍 Google Maps-based lead discovery
- 🏢 Extract relevant business information
- 📊 Automatically structure collected lead data
- 🔄 Process multiple leads through an automated workflow
- 📋 Organize leads into a usable format
- ⚡ Reduce repetitive manual prospecting
- 🤖 n8n-based workflow orchestration

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Enter the **target location**.
2. Enter the **type of business** you want to find.
3. The n8n workflow processes the search.
4. Relevant businesses are collected.
5. Lead information is extracted and structured.
6. The resulting leads are organized into a ready-to-use list.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **n8n** — Workflow automation & orchestration
- **Google Maps** — Business discovery
- **APIs / Data Extraction** — Lead data collection
- **Google Sheets** — Lead organization & storage

## 📈 Use Cases

This automation can be used for:

- B2B lead generation
- Local business prospecting
- Sales outreach
- Agency client acquisition
- Market research
- Local business database building

## 💡 Problem Solved

Traditional lead generation often requires sales teams to manually:

- Search for businesses
- Open individual listings
- Collect business information
- Copy data into spreadsheets
- Repeat the process for hundreds of businesses

This workflow automates the repetitive part of that process so the sales team can focus more on **qualification and outreach** rather than manual lead collection.
